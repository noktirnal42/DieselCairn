# Vehicle identity and configuration

## Known from the owner

| Field | Current record | Confidence / next evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Model year | 2009 | Owner-provided; confirm with VIN/title |
| Side badge / series | International RE300 | Owner observation |
| School-bus model designation | PB305 | Owner correction; preserve alongside RE300 |
| Engine | MaxxForce DT | Owner-provided; obtain engine serial/rating label |
| Transmission | Allison 3000 Series 3000PTS; electronic push-button selector and LED display | Owner-reported model; confirm nameplate. Serial, TCM generation, calibration, installed gear hardware, and configured maximum range remain unknown |
| VIN | Not supplied in this workspace | Enter locally; decode suggestions must be user-confirmed |

Do not collapse **RE300** and **PB305** into a single label: preserve RE300 as the bus series/badge and PB305 as the school-bus model designation. Record Allison **3000 Series 3000PTS** as owner-reported until checked against the nameplate. A VIN decoder may identify model-year/manufacturer attributes, but cannot prove the installed replacement engine, ECM, transmission tag, TCM, calibration, or six-speed capability.

## Collect these records before target-specific work

1. Full VIN and photograph of the chassis/body identification plate.
2. Engine serial, engine family/rating label, ECM part/serial, software and calibration/CIN identifiers.
3. Transmission nameplate model and serial; selector/control panel photograph.
4. TCM part/serial, generation, software/calibration identifiers and any service report.
5. Diagnostic connector photographs and wiring/service diagrams matching the VIN/build date.
6. Existing active/inactive DTC reports and calibration history.

Store locally by default. VIN decode results are suggestions until reviewed and corrected by the owner.
